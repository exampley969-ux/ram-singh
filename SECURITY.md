# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability
Email **exampley969@gmail.com** (TODO(owner): replace with a monitored security address) with:
what you found, steps to reproduce, and the affected URL or file. Please do not include other people's personal data, and do not
access, modify or delete data that is not yours.

What to expect (targets, not guarantees): acknowledgement within 3 working days, an initial assessment within 10 working days,
and a note when it is fixed. Please give us a reasonable time to fix before publishing details. Good-faith research that follows
these rules will not be treated as hostile.

In scope: this web app, its Supabase policies (`psyche-core/supabase/*.sql`), and the deployed site.
Out of scope: Supabase, Render and Google themselves (report to them), social engineering, denial-of-service load tests.

See `docs/SECURITY.md` for the threat model and `docs/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md` for how reports are handled.
