Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Psyche Core is a personality-assessment tool. This page explains what information it handles and why. It is written in plain language.

Using it without an account

You can take the assessment without signing in. In that case your answers are scored in your browser, and your result is stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

If you create an account or sign in

Accounts are provided through Supabase. When you sign up or sign in we handle:

  • Your email address, and your password if you sign up with email and password (stored by Supabase in hashed form; we never see it in plain text).
  • If you use Google sign-in: your Google account email address and basic profile information that Google shares with us (such as your name). We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, contacts, or other Google data.
  • Your computed result: your dimension scores, facet scores and archetype match. We do not store your individual answers to the 40 questions.

Your saved result is protected by access rules so that only your own signed-in account can read it.

Compatibility and groups (sharing with other people)

If you use Compatibility or Group analysis, we also store a small profile: your display name, your email address, your five dimension scores, your 20 facet scores (the narrower traits behind them) and your archetype. This profile is visible only to people you have chosen to connect with:

  • Connections: someone you invited (by email or share link) who accepted, or someone whose invitation you accepted.
  • Groups: everyone who has accepted membership of a group you have also accepted membership of. Joining a group shares your profile with all its members, including people who join later.

Nobody can see your scores before an invitation is accepted. Invitations store the sender's name and email and, for email invitations, the invited email address. You can remove a connection, leave a group, or decline an invitation at any time, which immediately stops that person seeing your profile. Your individual answers are never shared.

How the information is used

We use it only to let you sign in and to show you your saved result on any device. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

Supabase (authentication and database), Google (if you choose Google sign-in), and Render (website hosting) process data on our behalf to operate the service.

Your choices

  • You can delete your saved result at any time from the Account page; this also removes your scores from your shared profile.
  • To have your account removed entirely, contact us at the address below.
  • You can clear the copy stored in your browser by clearing site data.

Not professional advice

Psyche Core is not a medical, psychological or diagnostic tool. Results are for self-reflection only.
