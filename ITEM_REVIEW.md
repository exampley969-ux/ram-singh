## Summary

- Items with at least one flag: 22 of 40; medium severity: 14.
- Flag counts: double_barrelled 7, absolute_wording 6, construct_overlap 6, not_true_opposite 4, leading_or_desirable 4, sensitive_wording 1, idiom_or_cultural 1.
- Reading level: all items are short (mean grade 3.9, formula is crude for sentences this short). Reading difficulty is not the main risk; wording and construct overlap are.
- Design pattern: every facet has one + and one − item, always in the order (+, −) on adjacent screens. Order and adjacency effects are untested; randomised or interleaved order should be piloted.
- Overlap candidates to test with real data: R.adaptability / M.discipline (items 13, 14, 35), M.drive / M.ambition (33, 34, 37, 38), R.tempo / R.adaptability (9, 13), P.curiosity / M.persistence (3, 40).
